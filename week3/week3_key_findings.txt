The logistic regression model achieved an overall accuracy of 86.69%.
The model achieved a ROC-AUC of 0.8384, indicating good ability to distinguish between employees who stayed and employees who left.
Specificity was 95.93%, showing that the model was highly effective at identifying employees who did not leave.
Sensitivity was 38.30%, indicating that identifying actual employees who left remains the main area for model improvement.
Precision was 64.29%, meaning that approximately 64% of employees predicted to leave actually belonged to the attrition group.
The F1 Score was 48.00%, showing that the model has room for improvement in balancing precision and recall.
The model used multiple employee characteristics including age, business travel, job role, job satisfaction, monthly income, overtime, work-life balance and employment history.
ROC analysis, confusion matrix analysis and variable-importance analysis were used to evaluate and interpret the model.
